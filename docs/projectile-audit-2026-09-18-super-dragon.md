# Projectile audit — Super Dragon (`super_dragon`)

The dated current table supplies range 3 and a 1.8-second aggregate attack
cycle. Supercell identifies Super Dragon attacks as ground-and-air area splash,
but does not publish Roast count, radius or timing. Core therefore emits a
shared logical primary-target projectile only; omitted splash victims are an
explicit lower bound, not a claim of single-target fidelity.
