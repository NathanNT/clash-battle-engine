# bowler projectile audit delta

The ordinary `bowler` emits two serialized fixed impact projectiles on the initial target ray. The official Super Bowler comparison establishes the second impact by describing its third impact as one extra bounce. A dated secondary build describes the second building behind the initial target and the three-tile initial range.

The Core treats that three-tile range as a replaceable forward-step proxy. It uses zero splash radius rather than inventing an unsourced radius. Bounce spacing, collision, flight, loss of intermediate targets, walls, and timing remain open.
