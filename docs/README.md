`cocsim hero-support-catalogue` prints the shared Core's read-only Pet and
Equipment inventory and sourced Active/Passive type as JSON. The Viewer shows the same records in its
"Pets et equipements (catalogue seulement)" panel, and the RL binding exposes
`hero_support_catalogue()`. These entries are not combat actors yet.

The Core also exposes a read-only Monolith Arrow deployed-housing, tier and
14/10/5 maximum-HP percentage projection through Headless, Viewer and RL. Its Equipment class is Passive
with [dated secondary provenance](../data/reference/th18-2026-09-23-monolith-arrow-passive-type-override.json);
its projectile and damage effects are not active.

Scenarios and replays may carry `hero_loadouts`, with a selected Hero, one Pet
and up to two eligible Equipment items at explicit catalogue levels. Core
validates the selection, and Headless, Viewer and RL expose it as a value.
Selection does not activate Pet or Equipment combat behavior yet.
Snapshots require the same selected loadout to restore; logical state hashes
omit the selection until it can affect future combat ticks.

The Viewer has a prebattle loadout editor for its selected Heroes. Applying a
draft restarts the fixed-tick Core battle at T+0; the editor does not implement
Pet or Equipment effects. Its Grand Warden control selects the source-backed
ground or air `army.mode` before battle; replay playback retains that Core
scenario value.

`cocsim hero-support-level <id> <level>` and RL `hero_support_level(id, level)`
return the exact normalized content and level record, including the pinned
provenance. The Viewer can expand a selected Pet or Equipment level record.
These are immutable Core catalogue values; they do not apply combat effects.
The Viewer mouse tests exercise representative Pet and Equipment picker clicks;
its replay bridge preserves a selected loadout through fixed-tick playback.
Headless and RL runtime validators also check maximum-level records and
scenario selections for all 12 Pets and 42 Equipment items. Headless compares
22 one-Hero fixed-tick simulations with their value-replay output and hashes.
Royal Gem active-heal values remain a documented source gap in the
[dated recheck](../data/reference/th18-2026-09-23-royal-gem-active-heal-source-recheck.json);
its read-only HP/DPS records do not enable an Equipment combat effect.

RL scenario dictionaries preserve Grand Warden ground/air `army.mode` and
Hero Banner `non_combat_obstacles.variant`. RL observations return Core's
`flying` state and the selected Banner variant. These value paths do not
define the unsourced Hero Banner defensive behavior.

`docs/` describes durable systems and references. `state/` describes what is
currently changing. Detailed validation output must be generated by tools; it
must not overload `state/current.md`.
